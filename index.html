Features

Everything a DX hunter needs.

Built from the ground up for amateur radio operators. No bloat, just the data you need — fast, clean, and native iOS.

📡

CRX Network

Live DX spots via the CRX cluster API with automatic Telnet fallback. Stay connected even when the primary feed is down.

🔔

Watchlist Alerts

Add callsigns or DXCC prefixes to your watchlist. Get a push notification the moment your target appears in the spot stream.

🗂

Band & Mode Filters

Filter by band, mode, continent or DXCC entity. Quickly slice through hundreds of spots to find exactly what matters.

🌍

DXCC Lookup

Instant DXCC entity lookup for every spotted callsign. Flag, prefix, CQ zone and ITU zone shown at a glance.

📻

All HF Bands

160 metres through 10 metres, plus all three WARC bands. CW, SSB, FT8, RTTY — every mode in one unified stream.

✦

Native iOS Design

Built with SwiftUI for iPhone and iPad. Dark mode, Dynamic Type, background refresh and clean Apple design guidelines throughout.
